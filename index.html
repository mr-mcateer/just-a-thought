How it works

Four steps, every week.

01

Your hand

We collect the notes you have already written and learn your letters. The pen on the paper is real ink.

02

We listen to your recorded calls and learn how you talk: the phrases you use, the goals you mention, how you sign off.

03

Every week

We sit down with you for a short check-in on what is working and what is not. That conversation becomes this week's notes.

04

Private
